# Independent Show Handling Policy (Internal Ops)

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-24
**Authority:** JP (Editor-in-Chief)

## 1. Core Philosophy
CMP is a platform for **Independent Journalism and Storytelling**. We are NOT a "free speech absolutist" zone, nor are we a sanitized corporate PR outlet. We exist in the tension between "free expression" and "community safety."

**The Golden Rule:** We allow uncomfortable truths and sharp opinions. We DO NOT allow harm.

## 2. Editorial Authority
- **JP (Editor-in-Chief)** has final "Kill Switch" authority on ANY piece of content hosted on CMP servers.
- **Producer Autonomy:** Independent hosts own their IP and their editorial direction, *within* the boundaries of this policy.
- **CMP Branding:** CMP reserves the right to remove its branding from any show that drifts from our values, even if we continue to host it (or visa-versa).

## 3. Content Classifications

### ✅ Green: Allowed & Encouraged
- Political opinion (even radical ones).
- Investigative reporting on local power structures.
- Cultural commentary / Arts / Music.
- Religious programming (provided it is not exclusionary/hateful).

### ⚠️ Yellow: Review Required (Escalate to Admin)
- Content naming private citizens in a negative light (Defamation risk).
- Content involving minors (under 18) in *any* capacity.
- Content discussing active criminal cases (Contempt of court / interference risk).
- Medical advice/claims.

### ⛔ Red: Strictly Prohibited (Immediate Ban)
- **Hate Speech:** Slurs, dehumanization, calls for violence.
- **Doxxing:** Revealing private info (addresses, phone numbers) of individuals.
- **Pornography/Obscenity.**
- **Copyright Infringement.**

## 4. Governance Mechanics

### The "Safety Gate"
All independent uploads must pass a "Safety Gate" checklist before publishing.
- **Copyright Clear?** (Y/N)
- **Releases Signed?** (Y/N)
- **Minors Involved?** (Y/N)

### Retraction & Correction
- If a host is found to have published false info, they MUST issue a correction.
- Refusal to correct false info is grounds for show cancellation.

### De-Platforming
CMP reserves the right to remove any show, at any time, for any reason. We are a private publisher, not a government entity.
