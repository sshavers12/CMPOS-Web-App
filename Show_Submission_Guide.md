# How to Get Your Show on CMP: A Submission Guide

Welcome creator. The Chester Media Project (CMP) is looking for voices that are authentic, brave, and rooted in our community. We handle the tech and the platform; you bring the fire.

Here is how to get your show approved and live.

## 1. The Deal
We are not YouTube. We are a curated network.
- **We host:** Independent podcasts, video series, and community journalism.
- **We provide:** Hosting, distribution, and a "home base" operational dashboard.
- **You provide:** Content that respects our community.

## 2. What We Are Looking For
We want shows that:
- **Tell the Truth:** Even if it’s uncomfortable.
- **Respect Dignity:** Disagreement is fine; dehumanization is not.
- **Are Local:** Stories that matter to Chester and our neighbors.

## 3. The "No-Go" Zone (Immediate Rejection)
Don't bother submitting if your content contains:
- **Hate Speech:** Attacks on race, religion, gender, or identity.
- **Dangerous Disinfo:** Lies that can get people hurt.
- **Exploitation:** Using minors or vulnerable people without full, informed consent.
- **Copyright theft:** Using music or video you don't own.

## 4. The Submission Process (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: The Brief
Fill out the "New Show Brief" in the Partner Portal.
- **Pitch us:** What is the show in one sentence?
- **Show us:** Link to a pilot, a script, or a raw recording.

### Step 2: The Safety Check
You will answer a few mandatory questions about safety, consent, and rights. Be honest. If we find out you lied later, your show gets pulled immediately.

### Step 3: Review
Our team (and JP, the Editor-in-Chief) reviews every brief.
- **Green Light:** You get access to the Host Dashboard!
- **Yellow Light:** "Good idea, but fix X, Y, and Z first."
- **Red Light:** "Not a fit for CMP."

### Step 4: Production
Once approved, you upload episodes. We may spot-check content before it goes live.

## 5. Your Responsibilities
- **Get Releases:** If you interview someone, they MUST sign a release. No release = no airtime.
- **Be Accurate:** If you make a factual claim about a person or business, have the receipts.
- **Own Your Words:** You are independent, but you represent the network.

Ready? Go to the **Partner Portal** and click **"Submit Brief"**.
