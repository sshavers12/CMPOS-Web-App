# Escalation Rules: When to Call JP

**Purpose:** To protect the network from legal, reputational, and safety risks.
**Users:** Ops Team, AI Agents, Volunteer Moderators.

## 🚨 LEVEL 1: IMMEDIATE INTERVENTION (Wake JP Up)
*Stop everything. Pull the content. Call JP.*

1.  **Credible Threats of Violence:** Any content suggesting reliable intent to harm self or others.
2.  **Child Safety Incident:** Any suspicion of exploitation, lack of consent, or unauthorized filming of a minor.
3.  **Legal Threat:** Receipt of a "Cease and Desist" regarding an active show.
4.  **Live Doxxing:** Real-time broadcasting of private addresses/info.

## 🟠 LEVEL 2: COMPLIANCE BLOCKER (Pause & Review)
*Do not publish. Flag for Admin Queue (24hr SLA).*

1.  **Missing Release Forms:** Guest appears on camera/audio but no signature is tracked.
2.  **Copyright Flag:** Automated system detects licensed music (>15s).
3.  **Defamation Risk:** Host accuses a private citizen of a crime without citation/evidence.
4.  **Sponsorship Conflict:** Host is advertising a product that conflicts with CMP ethics (e.g., predatory loans, gambling).

## 🟡 LEVEL 3: EDITORIAL CONSULT (FYI)
*Publishing can proceed, but flag for post-air review.*

1.  **Tone/Quality Check:** Audio quality is poor, or branding is off.
2.  **Subject Matter Sensitivity:** Complex political topic that might cause backlash, but is not "hateful."
3.  **Duplicate Content:** Similar topic covered by another show recently.

## 🟢 LEVEL 4: AUTOMATED / STANDARD
*No human intervention needed.*

1.  Standard scheduled episodes from trusted "Green" tier hosts.
2.  Routine promo pack requests.
3.  Guest booking confirmations.
