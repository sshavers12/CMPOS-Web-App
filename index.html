CMP exists to build trusted community media infrastructure in Chester. We amplify authentic local narratives and create pathways for neighbors to inform, connect, and lead positive change.

MISSION Constantly Making Progress Cultivating Mature Positivity
THE ORIGIN STORY
"Chester had a narrative controlled by outsiders, no platform for its own voice, and no organized framework for transforming pain into purpose."

We solve the problem of disconnected communities lacking trusted information flow. We are the infrastructure for self-determination.
