# Weekly Reporting Cadence

**Objective:** Institutionalize learning. We don't just "post and pray"; we measure and adjust.

## 1. THE RHYTHM
*   **Data Pull:** Every Monday Morning (9:00 AM)
*   **Report Generation:** Monday Noon (12:00 PM)
*   **Review Meeting:** Tuesday Standup (15 mins)

---

**Subject:** `[CMP] Weekly Impact Report - Week of {Date}`

---

## 3. MEASUREMENT DEFINITIONS
*   **Views:** 3-second minimum (filter out skimmers).
*   **Watch Time:** The only metric that matters for algorithm health.
*   **CTR:** Clicks on "Link in Bio" or "Related Video".
*   **Subscriber Adds:** High-intent conversion.
