# Promotion Playbook

**Objective:** Define the strategy for maximizing reach across platforms using a single source asset.

## 1. STRATEGY: THE "FULL CIRCLE" FUNNEL
Content is not just for engagement; it is for **migration**.
1.  **Hook (Shorts/Reels):** Grab attention on rented land (Socials).
2.  **Value (Clip Body):** Deliver a complete thought or emotional moment.
3.  **Bridge (CTA):** "Watch the full story here" -> Drives to YouTube Longform.
4.  **Community (YouTube):** Convert Viewer to Subscriber -> Donor/Volunteer.

---

## 2. PLATFORM PACKS

### A. YOUTUBE SHORTS (The Growth Engine)
*   **Goal:** New Subscriber Acquisition.
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Title:** Declarative, Searchable (e.g., "Why Chester Needs Change"). Max 60 chars optimal.
    *   **Description:** 1-sentence summary + "Related Video" link enabled (Connect to longform).
    *   **Tags:** Broad categories (e.g., #Community #Activism #Faith).
    *   **Vibe:** Fast, information-dense, authoritative.

### B. INSTAGRAM REELS (The Brand Builder)
*   **Goal:** Deepen Relationship + Shareability.
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Visuals:** Must be aesthetic. Cover image matters.
    *   **Caption:** Longer form "Micro-blog". Tell a story in the text that compliments the video.
    *   **Hashtags:** 3-5 Niche tags blended into the text or first comment.
    *   **Sticker Strategy:** Use "Add Yours", Polls, or Location Tags to boost algorithm reach.

### C. TIKTOK (The Virality Wildcard)
*   **Goal:** Massive Awareness (Top of Funnel).
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Hook:** Must happen in 0.5 seconds.
    *   **Sound:** Use trending audio (low volume) behind the voice.
    *   **Caption:** Very short. Questions work best (e.g., "Do you agree?").
    *   **SEO:** Hashtags are critical here. Use 4-5 mixed (Broad + Local).

### D. FACEBOOK REELS (The Local Network)
*   **Goal:** Reach older demographic + Community Leaders.
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Content:** Focus on "Good News", "Inspiration", and "Local History".
    *   **Sharing:** Tag local partners/churches directly in the post.
    *   **Links:** Links in comments (Facebook suppresses posts with links in the body).

## 3. CAPTION & CTA RULES

### The "3-Line" Caption Formula
1.  **The Hook Line:** A punched-up version of the video hook.
2.  **The Context:** Why this matters right now.
3.  **The Call:** Specific instruction.

> *Example:*
> "Stop waiting for permission to lead. 🛑
> Pastor Rob breaks down why local action beats national noise every time.
> Watch the full episode on our channel (Link in Bio!) 🔗 #CMP #Chester"
